    How to play DNUP

    1. Goal: be the first to play your last card. Everyone starts with 7.
    2. The pile always has a direction: UP ▲ or DOWN ▼.
    3. On your turn, play a card that either follows the direction (higher than the top card when UP, lower when DOWN), matches the color, or matches the number.
    4. A number match flips the direction — that's the DNUP move.
    5. A 10 always turns the game DOWN ▼; a 1 always turns it UP ▲.
    6. Nothing to play (or holding back)? Draw one card — your turn ends either way. When the draw pile runs dry the discards are reshuffled; if there is truly nothing left to draw, you pass.
    7. Down to one card and the table calls DNUP! for you. Play it next turn to win.
    8. Deck: numbers 1–10 in four colors (two of each), 80 cards. 2–5 players.
