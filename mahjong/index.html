    How to play Mahjong

    1. The tiles: 136 tiles — three number suits (Characters, Circles, Bamboo, each 1–9 × 4 copies), four Winds (× 4), and three Dragons (× 4). Hong Kong and Taiwanese rules add 8 Flower/Season tiles (144 total).
    2. The deal: each player gets 13 tiles (16 in Taiwanese). The dealer (East) draws first.
    3. Your turn: draw a tile from the wall, then discard one. Your goal is to form a winning hand.
    4. Winning hand: 4 sets + 1 pair (5 sets + 1 pair in Taiwanese). A set is three consecutive tiles of one suit (Chi), three identical tiles (Pon), or four identical tiles (Kan).
    5. Claiming discards:
      · Chi — take the discard to complete a sequence (only from the player to your left).
      · Pon — take the discard to complete a triplet (from anyone).
      · Kan — take the discard to complete a quad (from anyone). Draw a replacement tile.
      · Ron — the discard completes your winning hand (from anyone).
      Priority: Ron > Pon/Kan > Chi.
    6. Tsumo: if the tile you draw completes your hand, declare Tsumo to win.
    7. Variant rules:
      · Hong Kong: 3-faan minimum to win. Faan from winds, dragons, flushes, flowers.
      · Japanese Riichi: must have at least 1 yaku. Declare Riichi when 1 tile away from winning (bet 1000 pts, lock your hand). Dora tiles add bonus han. Furiten: cannot Ron if you discarded a tile you could win on.
      · Taiwanese: 16-tile hand, 5 sets + 1 pair. Flowers drawn aside for bonuses. Tai-based scoring.
    8. Scoring: the winner earns points based on the hand's value. Tsumo: all others pay. Ron: the discarder pays.
