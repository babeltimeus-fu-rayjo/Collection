    How to play Skull King

    1. Ten rounds: round N deals N cards. Everyone secretly bids how many tricks they'll win (0 to N); bids are revealed together — yo, ho, ho! With 8 players the 70-card deck runs short, so rounds 9 and 10 deal 8 cards each (per the official note).
    2. Following suit: the first number played sets the suit, and you must follow it if you can. The black Jolly Roger suit beats the other three — but it's still a suit: you can only play black when you're out of the led colour (or when black leads). Special cards may be played anytime.
    3. Specials: Pirates beat all numbers. The Skull King beats the pirates. A Mermaid beats the Skull King — even if pirates are there too. Between equals, first played wins. The Tigress is played as a pirate or an escape — your choice, declared as you play her.
    4. Escapes always lose — that's the point. If everyone escapes, the first card takes the trick.
    5. Leads: a pirate, mermaid or Skull King lead makes the whole trick suit-free. An escape lead passes suit-setting to the next player.
    6. Scoring — the host picks one of the three official systems in the lobby:
      · Skull King's scoring (classic): hit your bid exactly for +20 per trick; miss it for −10 per trick off. A zero bid pays ±10 × the cards dealt — brave in round nine.
      · The Rascal's scoring: every round is worth 10 × the cards dealt for everyone, whatever they bid. A direct hit earns all of it, off-by-one earns half, off by 2+ earns nothing. Never negative.
      · Cannonball: 15 × the cards dealt on a direct hit — and nothing at all if you're off, even by one.
    7. Bonuses (with an exact bid — or halved on a Rascal's glancing blow): each captured 14 is +10 (the black 14 is +20); the Skull King earns +30 per pirate played before him; capturing the Skull King with a mermaid is +50.
    8. Highest total after round ten rules the seas.

    Skull King is by Brent Beck (© Grandpa Beck's Games). This unofficial fan implementation uses the modern 70-card deck — four suits of 1–14, five pirates, five escapes, two mermaids, the Tigress, and the King himself. The Legendary expansion menu (Loot, Kraken, White Whale) is not included.
